# Resume Tailor

Language for discovering jobs, preparing application materials, and tracking
employer responses.

## Language

**Job**:
A particular employment opportunity identified by its employer, role, and posting.
_Avoid_: Application, when referring to the opportunity itself.

**Application**:
The applicant's pursuit of a Job, including its preparation, submission, and
employer response history. An Application may exist before it is submitted.
_Avoid_: Job, when referring to the applicant's progress.

**Job description**:
The posting text describing an employment opportunity and its requirements.
It is employer-provided context, not evidence about the applicant.
_Avoid_: Applicant evidence.

**Connected mailbox**:
An email account connected to the workspace for reading recruiting messages,
with a selected folder where the provider supports that choice.
_Avoid_: User account, employer account.

**Application update proposal**:
A suggested change to an Application's employer-response status, supported by
an incoming message and awaiting the user's review.
_Avoid_: Applied update, confirmed decision before acceptance.
