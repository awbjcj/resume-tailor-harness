# Resume Tailor

Language for discovering jobs, preparing application materials, and tracking
employer responses.

## Language

**Job**:
A particular employment opportunity identified by its employer, role, and posting.
_Avoid_: Application, when referring to the opportunity itself.

**Application**:
The applicant's pursuit of a Job, including its preparation, submission, and
employer response history. An Application may exist before it is submitted.
_Avoid_: Job, when referring to the applicant's progress.

**Job description**:
The posting text describing an employment opportunity and its requirements.
It is employer-provided context, not evidence about the applicant.
_Avoid_: Applicant evidence.

**Connected mailbox**:
An email account connected to the workspace for reading recruiting messages,
with a selected folder where the provider supports that choice.
_Avoid_: User account, employer account.

**Application update proposal**:
A suggested change to an Application's employer-response status, supported by
an incoming message and awaiting the user's review.
_Avoid_: Applied update, confirmed decision before acceptance.

**Application next step**:
The work still needed to prepare or track an Application, considering the Job,
the chosen materials, and any confirmed submission history.
_Avoid_: Job status, when describing what the applicant should do next.

**Profile publication**:
A successful incorporation of saved applicant evidence into the facts available
for tailoring. Saving new evidence alone does not make it part of that publication.
_Avoid_: Save time, when describing whether evidence has been incorporated.

**Saved evidence**:
Applicant evidence retained as a source. It may still be awaiting incorporation
into a Profile publication.
_Avoid_: Published fact, before the evidence has been incorporated.

**Profile refresh**:
An explicitly requested attempt to incorporate Saved evidence into a new Profile
publication. A failed or skipped attempt leaves the saved evidence available.
_Avoid_: Save, when referring to rebuilding the usable profile.

**Application preparation**:
The chosen materials and confirmed applicant answers carried into work on a Job's
application form. Preparation does not mean the Application was submitted.
_Avoid_: Applied, when referring only to selected materials.

**Run result**:
The outcome of background work and the work it belongs to, available for the
applicant to revisit after leaving and returning to the workspace.
_Avoid_: Notification read state, when describing whether the next work is done.
